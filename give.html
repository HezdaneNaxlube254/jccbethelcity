Giving FAQ

What is a tithe?

A tithe is 10% of your income, given as an act of worship and obedience to God. It's the biblical foundation of giving that builds the church and reaches the lost.

What is an offering?

An offering is giving above and beyond the tithe. It's a way to sow abundantly into special projects, missionary work, or community aid.

Can I set up recurring giving?

Yes! You can set up automatic transfers via M-Pesa standing orders or bank standing orders. Consistency in giving trains your heart and ensures the church can plan ahead.

Is my giving secure?

Absolutely. We use secure platforms for all online giving. Your financial information is protected and never shared.

Do I get a receipt for my giving?

Yes, receipts are provided for all giving. Please contact the finance team for any documentation you may need for tax purposes.

How can I pray for my offering?

Before you give, pray and ask God to multiply your seed and use it for His glory. Your offering is an act of worship—let it be accompanied by prayer.
