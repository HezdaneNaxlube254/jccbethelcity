Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our church.

What do I wear to church?

Come as you are. We have no dress code. Wear what makes you comfortable. We focus on the heart, not the outfit. Whether you prefer formal attire or casual clothes, you are welcome here.

Is it okay if I'm new?

Absolutely! You are not an accident, and your presence is not a mistake. We have a dedicated team to welcome first-time visitors. You'll be loved and cared for from the moment you walk through our doors.

Where do I park?

Parking is available on the church grounds. If you have mobility challenges, please let us know—we have designated spaces close to the entrance. Our ushers will be happy to assist you.

Do you have childcare?

Yes! We have Sunday School for children aged 3-12 during the Sunday service. Our trained, loving teachers create a safe, faith-filled environment where your children can learn about God's love through Bible stories, activities, and prayer.

How do I join a ministry?

Talk to one of our ushers, visit the ministries page, or fill out our contact form. We'll connect you with leaders in your area of interest. There's a place for everyone to serve!

What if I have questions?

Ask away! We have pastors and leaders available before and after service, or reach out via phone/WhatsApp. No question is too small. We're here to help you on your faith journey.

Can I watch services online?

Yes! We offer live streaming of our Sunday services on our YouTube channel. You can also watch past sermons anytime. Stay connected even when you can't be with us in person.

How can I give or tithe?

You can give via M-Pesa Paybill 588344 (Account: Reason for Giving), bank transfer, or during our services. Every gift, no matter the size, helps us reach more lives with the gospel.
