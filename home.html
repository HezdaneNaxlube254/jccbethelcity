We are a family.
Not a crowd.

JCC Bethel City is more than a Sunday service. We are a community of believers united by faith, purpose, and genuine love for one another. For over a decade, we have been growing in the power of the Holy Spirit, reaching into Athi River and beyond with the message of Jesus Christ's salvation and transformation.

Led by Pst. Rodgers Silali and Pastor Jacklyn , our church has become a beacon of hope—a place where broken people encounter wholeness, where the lost find their way home, and where the ordinary becomes extraordinary through faith.

Whether you are here for the first time or have been part of our journey for years, you belong here. Let's grow together.

Led By Vision, Rooted In Love

Mr & Mrs Rodgers Silali

Senior Pastors & Founders of JCC Bethel City

Together, Pst. Rodgers and Pastor Jacklyn have dedicated their lives to building a church that reflects the heart of God—a place of prayer, power, and authentic community. With over a decade of ministry in Athi River, they have seen countless lives transformed by the gospel. Their partnership in marriage and ministry is a testament to God's faithfulness, and their love for the people of JCC Bethel City is evident in every service, every prayer, and every act of service.

"The church is not a building—it's a family. When we embrace our identity as Christ's body, the world has no choice but to witness the resurrection power of Jesus."
