Women's Ministry

Empowering women through the Word and community

The Women's Ministry is a safe haven for women of all ages and backgrounds. We gather to pray, study Scripture, grow in our identity as daughters of God, and support one another through life's joys and challenges. Under the shepherding care of Pastor Jacklyn Mucholwa and our ministry leaders, we are discovering our divine assignment and walking in confidence, strength, and grace.

"This ministry has transformed my life. I've found sisters who pray with me, laugh with me, and walk with me through every season."

— Mary W., Ministry Member

Leader: Pastor Jacklyn & Team

Meetings: Second Saturday of each month | 2:00 PM

Men's Ministry

Building godly men of character and purpose

The Men's Ministry calls men to a higher standard of faith, family, and community. We gather for prayer, spiritual accountability, discipleship, and strategic action to reach our families and our city with the gospel. Led by Rev. Rodgers Silali, this is where men become the spiritual leaders God designed them to be—strong, faithful, and on mission.

"Being part of this men's group has made me a better husband, father, and leader. The accountability and prayer support is unmatched."

— David K., Ministry Member

Leader: Pst. Rodgers Silali & Team

Meetings: First Saturday of each month | 10:00 AM

Welfare Ministry

Practical support for families in need

Jesus taught that whatever we do for the least among us, we do for Him. The Welfare Ministry identifies families and individuals facing financial hardship, food insecurity, medical emergencies, or educational barriers. We provide practical, dignified support—not hand-outs, but hand-ups—that restore hope and enable people to thrive. Your giving flows through this channel to touch lives that are facing genuine crisis.

"When our family was going through a difficult time, the Welfare Ministry showed up with love, prayer, and practical help. We will never forget their kindness."

— Grace M., Ministry Beneficiary

Leader: Welfare Committee Team

How to Help: Contribute to the Welfare Fund or volunteer your skills

Dorcas Ministry

Hands-on community service and compassion

Named after the woman in Acts 9 who was known for her good works and almsgiving, the Dorcas Ministry embodies hands-on compassion. We sew, prepare meals, distribute care packages, visit the sick, and serve our community with genuine love. This is not a program—it's a posture. It's the lifestyle of a believer who has encountered God's kindness and cannot help but extend it to others.

"Serving with Dorcas Ministry has opened my eyes to the power of simple acts of love. Every meal we prepare, every visit we make, we see God's love in action."

— Sarah O., Ministry Volunteer

Leader: Dorcas Ministry Team

Meetings: Wednesdays | 4:00 PM

Praise & Worship Ministry

Leading the congregation into God's presence through music

Worship is not music—it's a lifestyle. Yet music is one of our most powerful tools to lead people into authentic encounter with God. Our Praise & Worship Ministry is a team of musicians, singers, and technical servants who labor to create a space where heaven and earth meet. Whether you can sing like an angel or carry a tune in a bucket, if your heart burns with worship, there is a place for you here.

"Leading worship has deepened my relationship with God in ways I never imagined. There's nothing like seeing people encounter His presence through song."

— James M., Worship Team Member

Leader: Worship Director & Band Team

Rehearsals: Fridays | 4:00 PM

Sunday School

Faith formation for children aged 3–12

The Sunday School Ministry is where the next generation encounters Jesus and builds a foundation of faith for their entire lives. Through Bible stories, interactive lessons, prayer, worship, and community, we help children know Jesus personally, love His Word, and discover their purpose in His kingdom. Parents, your children are not just being cared for—they are being discipled by a team that loves them as their own.

"My children look forward to Sunday School every week. The teachers are so loving, and I can see the Word of God coming alive in their little hearts."

— Elizabeth W., Parent

Leader: Sunday School Coordinator & Teachers

Schedule: Every Sunday | 9:00 AM (concurrent with main service)
