Our Story

A decade of faith, transformation, and community in Athi River.

Our Mission

To celebrate Jesus and transform communities through the power of the Holy Spirit, reaching every life in Athi River and beyond.

Our Vision

A church for every nation, tribe, and generation—where broken people find wholeness, the lost find home, and the ordinary becomes extraordinary through faith.

Our Journey

2014

The Birth of a Vision

Pst. Rodgers Silali received a divine mandate to establish a church rooted in prayer, power, and community transformation. JCC Bethel City was birthed in faith.

2016

First Sanctuary

We established our first dedicated sanctuary in Athi River. The community began to grow rapidly as word spread of the authentic move of God's Spirit.

2019

Expansion & Ministries

Recognizing the diverse needs of our community, we launched dedicated ministries: Women's, Men's, Welfare, Dorcas, Praise & Worship, and Sunday School.

2025

Global Reach

Today, JCC Bethel City stands as a beacon of hope in Athi River, with over 100 committed believers and an impact that extends beyond our borders through the JCC Network.

Our Core Values

Prayer

Prayer is the foundation of our church. We believe that authentic connection with God through prayer transforms hearts, heals communities, and unleashes God's power in our midst.

"Pray without ceasing." — 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Community

We are not a crowd; we are a family. Community means authentic relationships, shared burdens, and celebrating victories together. When one member thrives, we all thrive.

"They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to fellowship." — Acts 2:42

Generosity

Generosity is love in action. We give our time, talents, and treasures freely, knowing that God is faithful to multiply what we steward for His kingdom and His people.

"God loves a cheerful giver." — 2 Corinthians 9:7

Excellence

Excellence honors God. Whether in worship, service, or discipleship, we pursue excellence not for accolades, but because our God deserves our very best.

"Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart." — Colossians 3:23

JCC Network

Connecting churches across Kenya

Part of the JCC Network

JCC Bethel City is part of the Jesus Celebration Centre (JCC) network—a fellowship of Spirit-filled churches across Kenya united by a common vision: to celebrate Jesus and transform the continent through authentic discipleship and community impact.

Our connection to the broader JCC family strengthens our commitment to excellence, prophetic ministry, and social impact. Together with sister churches, we are witnessing revival that crosses denominational and ethnic boundaries.

Learn more about the JCC Network at jcckenya.org
